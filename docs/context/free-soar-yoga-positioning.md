# Free Soar Yoga Positioning

## Product Definition

Free Soar Yoga is the first-phase marketplace product under the Free Soar master brand.

It is a yoga group-class marketplace that helps:

- Organizers / group leaders create yoga class demand requests
- Yoga teachers discover and respond to suitable group-class opportunities
- Members enroll in class sessions
- Platform admins manage quality, safety, and operations

## Strategic Role

Free Soar Yoga is the first strong product entry point for the future Free Soar Life Platform.

It starts with yoga, but preserves future expansion into:

- Wellness
- Community
- Academy
- Retreat
- AI-driven services

## Marketplace Identity

Free Soar Yoga is not just:

- A yoga blog
- A teacher directory
- A generic booking system
- A discount class marketplace

It is:

- A brand-driven yoga marketplace
- A group-class coordination platform
- A teacher opportunity platform
- A community-oriented body-mind practice platform
