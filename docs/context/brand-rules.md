# Free Soar Brand Rules

## Master Brand

Free Soar 飛索 is the master brand and total entry point.

## Brand Core

自由飛翔 × 身心靈整合 × 女性成長 × AI 賦能 × 生活方式平台

## Mission

Help people rebuild a freer, more autonomous, more awakened life through body, mind, growth, community, and technology.

## Brand Essence

讓人找回內在力量，自由展翅。

## Core Values

- Freedom 自由
- Awakening 覺醒
- Growth 成長
- Wellness 身心整合
- Leadership 自主人生
- Community 共創社群

## Product Implication

Free Soar Yoga can be a marketplace, but it must not feel like a cold transaction platform.
It should feel like a high-quality, gentle, empowering, trustworthy life platform.

## Must Express

- Inner freedom
- Body-mind integration
- Gentle growth
- Teacher/community trust
- Modern spirituality
- AI-enabled efficiency without losing warmth

## Avoid

- Pure discount marketplace tone
- Course dumping without brand spirit
- Overly commercial or pushy copy
- Generic fitness website feeling
- Hard-selling wellness claims
- Unverified medical/therapeutic claims
