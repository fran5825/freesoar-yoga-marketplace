# Voice and Tone

## Voice

Free Soar Yoga speaks with a voice that is:

- Gentle but clear
- Grounded but inspiring
- Professional but warm
- Feminine but not exclusionary
- Spiritual but not vague
- Modern but not cold

## Tone Principles

Use:

- Calm confidence
- Spacious wording
- Clear guidance
- Trust-building language
- Simple language for complex flows

Avoid:

- Anxiety-driven marketing
- Overpromising transformation
- Hard-sell urgency
- Cheap discount phrasing
- Overly mystical claims
- Medical claims without evidence

## Example Phrases

Good:

- 「一起回到身體，重新找到安住與自由。」
- 「為公司社團與社區，找到適合的瑜伽老師。」
- 「讓開課、報名與排課變得更清楚、更安心。」
- 「從一堂課開始，建立可持續的身心練習社群。」

Avoid:

- 「最低價搶課」
- 「保證療癒所有問題」
- 「全網最便宜瑜伽」
- 「立即變美變瘦」
